Motion Publisher

A personal, self-hosted tool that publishes videos made with the owner's own Motion Studio to the owner's own social media channels.

What it does

  • Keeps a local library of rendered videos on the owner's computer.
  • Uploads a chosen video, with its title and description, to the owner's YouTube channel at a scheduled time.
  • Shows the status and link of each upload.

Who it is for

Motion Publisher is not a public service and has no sign-up. It runs only on its owner's computer and only connects to accounts the owner signs in with.
