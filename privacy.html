Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Motion Publisher is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It has no servers of its own and no other users.

Data it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube account through Google sign-in, Motion Publisher requests permission to upload and manage videos on that channel and to read basic channel and video information (such as titles, view counts and comments) for the owner's own reports.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos the owner chose, with the title and description the owner approved.
  • To show the owner the status, link and statistics of their own uploads.

Data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Google OAuth tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local database, with tokens encrypted. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Google API Services

Motion Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local Motion Publisher data folder removes all stored data.
